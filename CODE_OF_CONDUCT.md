# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the work, not the person. Assume the other party
is competent and acting in good faith until you have real evidence
otherwise.

## Scope

This applies to everyone taking part in this repository and its
surrounding spaces: issues, pull requests, code review, commit messages,
and any private correspondence that arises from them. It applies equally
to maintainers, contributors, contractors, and anyone operating an
automated or agent-driven account.

## Expected behavior

- Give and take criticism about code, design, and decisions directly and
  without dressing it up. Directness is welcome here.
- Aim criticism at the work. "This helper does not escape U+2028" is
  fine. "You are careless" is not.
- Explain your reasoning. A review comment that only says no is not
  useful to anyone.
- Accept a decision once it is made and recorded, and reopen it with new
  evidence rather than by relitigating it in a different thread.
- Respect the boundaries this project has written down. Disagreeing with
  a documented control is legitimate; quietly working around it is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained personal hostility.
- Discriminatory or demeaning remarks about anyone, including about
  characteristics that have nothing to do with the work.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately introducing a defect, a backdoor, or a dependency you
  know to be compromised.
- Attempting to bypass the repository's controls, or pressuring someone
  else to bypass them.
- Misrepresenting the state of a change: claiming a gate passed when it
  did not, that a check ran when it did not, or that a control is
  enforced when nothing enforces it.

That last point is not filler. The controls in this repository depend on
people reporting honestly, and a false claim of a passing gate is
treated as seriously as any other breach of this document. The same
applies to documentation: writing that a control is in place when it is
only configured is the same failure in a different medium.

## Reporting

Report a concern to `@oa` at `mail@fiona.sm`. Reports are handled
privately. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so plainly in the
message; it will still be read.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides on a response proportionate to what happened. It
may be a private word, a request for a public correction, reverting a
change, or withdrawing repository access. Decisions are explained to the
person concerned.
