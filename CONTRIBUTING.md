# Contributing

`AGENTS.md` is the engineering standard.
[docs/CODING-STANDARD.md](docs/CODING-STANDARD.md) and
[docs/VOICE.md](docs/VOICE.md) narrow it further. Read all three before
opening a change.

Commits are signed, under the identity `AGENTS.md` § Commits describes.

Run `brew style` before pushing a cask change. `gate:ci` and `gate:scan`
are the merge gates.
